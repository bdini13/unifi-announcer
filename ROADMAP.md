# Roadmap

## Planned

### Friendly preset management

- Add a web/API workflow to create, edit, and remove slot-backed presets.
- Store a user-facing name separately from the exact spoken text.
- Refresh Home Assistant preset choices without editing `.env` or restarting the container.
- Preserve the two fixed, service-owned TTS slots rather than allocating permanent Protect ringtones.

### Home Assistant media ingestion

- Native `tts.speak` support.
- `media-source://` and binary media ingestion.
